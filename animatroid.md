# Animatroid design

## Introduction

Animatroid, defined as a 3D-printed, intelligent mechanical life-form doll, will be a major product sold by XmechWorks. The design philosophy behind animatronics is rooted in 
their biomimetic appearance, intricate mechanical structures, and neural-network-driven intelligence. At first, these mechanical dolls will be designed by human experts with 
the assistance of artificial intelligence. Meanwhile, a generative design tool, i.e., EgoDesign, will be developed for a completely automatic design process.

## Design methodologies

* Modular design method
* Additive design method

### Capra Ibex

1. Use the AT89S52 as the main microcontroller;
2. Use additive and modular design methods;
3. Place the motors and gears together as a multi-DOF reducer, forming an integrated leg;
4. Each limb has four degrees of freedom.
